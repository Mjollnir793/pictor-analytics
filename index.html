No framework

No roadmap

Costly outcomes

Businesses over-invest in tools they never implement, or under-invest and fall behind competitors who do.

The Solution

A three-phase audit methodology — built in restaurants, deployable anywhere.

One of the most operationally complex, margin-thin sectors in the economy forced a framework that pays for itself fast and survives real-world constraints.

01

Diagnostic Assessment

A structured review of current operations across five categories: workforce management, inventory & cost control, customer experience, marketing automation, and back-office admin.

Output — a scored gap analysis with benchmark comparisons.

02

Opportunity Mapping

Each gap is mapped against available AI tools, with ROI modelling for time savings, cost reduction, or revenue lift — evaluated for complexity, cost, and fit.

Output — a prioritised opportunity matrix.

03

Implementation Roadmap

A 90-day action plan specifying which tools to implement, in what sequence, with what success metrics — plus vendor introductions and a staff adoption framework.

Output — a client-ready PDF roadmap, with an optional retainer for ongoing accountability.
